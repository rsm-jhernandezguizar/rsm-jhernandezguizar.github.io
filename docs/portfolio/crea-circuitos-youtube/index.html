Crea Circuitos: engineering knowledge in Spanish

From a technical education channel to a maintainable learning platform built around clear architectures and practical guides.
Author

Juan Hernández Guizar

Published

November 15, 2026

The problem

Junior developers and engineering students need more than tool tutorials: they need explanations that help them make and defend their own design decisions. Crea Circuitos addresses this need in Spanish, with global reach and relevance to Latin America.

My contribution

I am defining the platform vision, engineering principles, and learning approach, connecting my systems engineering background with technical communication and practical software workflows.

Architecture and decisions

  • Content first: maintain technical guides as versioned Markdown.
  • Separate audiences: distinguish the public learning website from the internal engineering guide.
  • Static delivery: baseline Astro and Cloudflare Pages for the public site at the purchased domain, creacircuitos.com.
  • Review before publication: validate changes through merge requests and CI.
  • Cost discipline: begin with free service tiers and local experiments rather than paid inference or subscriptions.

Evidence and current status

The engineering guide has a working MkDocs build and CI pipeline. The public Astro website, a local Markdown link-checking experiment, and an AI workflow evaluation protocol are under development and review. Public domain activation remains a deployment milestone; it is not presented here as completed.

The project demonstrates systems thinking, documentation architecture, engineering governance, and Spanish-language technical communication. Learning outcomes and community impact will be measured as content is published; no audience-growth or revenue claims are made at this stage.
